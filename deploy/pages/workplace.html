How we work

Remote-first.
Async by default.

Cellar is an early-stage, founder-led startup. We build with focus, flexibility, and customer impact—and document how we work as the team grows.

Our working principles

A clear default for thoughtful work.

We use these principles to guide day-to-day work and collaboration.

01

Remote by default

Work from where you can do your best work. We coordinate through shared tools and written context.

02

Async first

Share updates and decisions in writing. Use meetings when a live conversation will make the work clearer or faster.

03

Outcomes over optics

Make goals, ownership, and next steps clear. We care about useful progress and customer impact—not being online for show.

04

Sustainable pace

Plan work thoughtfully, respect personal time, and raise risks early so deadlines do not depend on constant urgency.

05

Build responsibly

Consider privacy and security in product decisions, and use customer data only when it is needed for the work.

This page describes Cellar’s current working approach. As the company grows, we’ll update it to reflect the team and applicable requirements.
