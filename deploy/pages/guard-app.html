Help with Cellar Guard

Find an answer or send the team a question.

Can I check a CSV without a JSON file?

Yes. Enter one required column name per line under Import rules, choose the delimiter, then tap Use these rules. Add your CSV file or paste its text and tap Check export.

Where do I find my CSV on a phone?

In your spreadsheet app, export or save a copy as CSV. Then use Choose CSV file to find it in Files or Downloads. You can also paste CSV text into the checker.

Why was my export blocked?

Open the failing integration to see the column name, record number, and suggested fix. A missing column means the export header differs from what the import rules require.

For a reply, include your email and the error message. Keep customer records out of the message.
