What we put first

Standards of work, not fake badges.

These are intentions and engineering practices. They are not claims of independent compliance testing, legal accreditation or company certification.

Accessibility

Make it usable.

Keep navigation understandable, support keyboard use, respect reduced-motion preferences and review visual contrast. This is a design commitment, not a WCAG certification.

Engineering

Show the evidence.

Document assumptions, maintain reproducible tests, make failures visible and distinguish prototypes from finished services.

Collaboration

Respect ownership.

Use scoped permissions, protect confidential material, credit contributors and preserve third-party licenses.

Responsible conduct

Do the right thing while building the new thing.

Share feedback respectfully.Contributions should address the work, explain the problem and avoid personal attacks or harassment.

Protect sensitive information.Do not submit secrets, private customer files, credentials or security vulnerabilities through public demonstration forms.

Make honest claims.Only publish verified credentials and real product capabilities. Unannounced ideas stay explicitly unannounced.

Keep learning

Principles get stronger with practice.

These public working policies are maintained alongside the project documentation. Formal customer policies and security reporting channels will need separate definition before relevant services launch.
