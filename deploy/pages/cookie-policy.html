Browser choices / reviewed 27 November 2026

Small files. Clear choices.

A cookie is not the same thing as browser local storage. Here is what the headquarters site actually stores and what your privacy choices control.

Privacy notice
LOCAL BY DEFAULT0×NO AD COOKIES IN SITE CODE

Current storage inventory

Only what's actually implemented.

The company site uses four local-storage keys: theme mode, your current custom palette, up to 12 saved custom palettes, and privacy choices. The source does not install advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting services may process requests separately.

Functional preference

Theme choice.

cellar-studio-theme remembers System, Light, Dark, or Custom mode. cellar-studio-custom-theme stores your active palette and settings, and cellar-studio-custom-library stores themes you choose to save. These stay in your browser.

Privacy choice

Your decision.

cellar-privacy-preferences stores whether you allowed optional third-party embeds and when that choice was saved.

External media

Off by default.

Optional embeds are not loaded by the consent module until you opt in. The current company pages have no active external iframe embeds.

Know the difference

Permission for embeds isn't permission for everything.

Essential storage.Theme and privacy settings remain available regardless of the optional embed choice. Clear them in your browser to reset.

Optional external embeds.Choosing “Allow embeds” authorizes future media widgets explicitly marked as optional; it does not enable advertising or analytics code.

Other network requests.The site currently uses Google Fonts and Vercel hosting, which can receive standard requests. The embed choice does not block font or hosting requests.

Your controls

Change your mind any time.

Use the Privacy settings control in this page or the footer to change optional embed preferences. You can also clear the two stored keys in your browser's site-data settings.
