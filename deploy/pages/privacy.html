The current website

Simple pages. Limited data needs.

This notice covers the informational pages and contact form on the Cellar Studio headquarters site. Separate applications, integrations and linked providers may have different data practices.

Accounts & payments

No account or checkout.

These pages do not ask visitors to register, sign in or enter payment details. No online purchases are processed.

Browser preferences

Your choice stays in-browser.

Local storage remembers cellar-studio-theme and cellar-privacy-preferences. Clear site storage to reset them.

Infrastructure

Hosting & fonts.

Vercel serves the site and may process standard request information. Google Fonts can receive requests when fonts load. Their terms govern those services.

Data inventory

What you enter. What we receive.

When you select Send message, your browser submits the form to FormSubmit. It forwards the message to Cellar Studio at cellarcsv@gmail.com. We use this information to respond to your enquiry.
